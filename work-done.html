2.1

What work is

If you drag a heavy box along the ground, you have to push or pull against friction to keep it moving. In mechanics we say you are doing work. Work is done on an object whenever a force acts on it and the object moves.

The work done by a force is the component of the force in the direction of motion × the distance moved. When the force acts along the line of motion, this is simply work done = force × distance, or W = Fs. With force in newtons and distance in metres, work is measured in joules (J).

Whenever a body's vertical height increases, work is done against gravity. Lifting a mass m through a vertical height h takes work done against gravity = mgh. It makes no difference whether the body goes straight up or along a slope: only the vertical height counts.

Example 1

Pulling a box

A box is pulled 7 m across a horizontal floor by a horizontal force of magnitude 15 N. Calculate the work done by the force.
Example 2

Finding a resistance

A packing case is pulled across a horizontal floor by a horizontal rope. The case moves at a constant speed and there is a constant resistance to motion of magnitude R newtons. When the case has moved a distance of 12 m the work done is 96 J. Calculate the magnitude of the resistance.
Example 3

Raising a load of bricks

A bricklayer raises a load of bricks of total mass 30 kg at a constant speed by attaching a cable to the bricks. Assuming the cable is vertical, calculate the work done when the bricks are raised a distance of 7 m.
Example 4

Up a rough slope

A package of mass 2 kg is pulled at a constant speed up a rough plane which is inclined at 30° to the horizontal. The coefficient of friction between the package and the surface is 0.35. The package is pulled 12 m up a line of greatest slope of the plane. Calculate:
  1. the work done against gravity
  2. the work done against friction.
Example 5

A force at an angle

A sledge is pulled 15 m across a smooth sheet of ice by a force of magnitude 27 N. The force is inclined at 25° to the horizontal. By modelling the sledge as a particle calculate the work done by the force.
